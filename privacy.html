Privacy

Analytics

This portfolio uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and improve the site. It records anonymous page-view statistics such as visited pages, broad device information, approximate location, and referring sources as provided by Vercel.

The site does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, raw search terms, IP addresses, cookies, or persistent cross-site identifiers through its custom analytics events. Project and link interactions use only a project slug and a destination category such as demo or repo.

External links

Links to services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, and project demos take you to those providers, which have their own privacy policies.

Contact
